# Sanctuary System

Back to [[00 - Project Dashboard]] · Not built until Phase 4.

## Initial layout
Small entrance · three preset habitats · connecting paths · decorative fences · basic landscaping · signs · small visitor area · clear safari departure point.
Compact — players see their animals without long walks.

## Habitats (MVP = preset structures)
| Species | Habitat |
|---|---|
| Zebra | Grassland |
| Elephant | Spacious savannah |
| Giraffe | Tall-tree grassland |

Befriend species → unlock placement → animal appears inside with simple idle movement.
**Not in MVP:** free-form building, pathfinding, needs/feeding, breeding, disease.

## Sanctuary Bonds (unique twist — exploratory)
Sanctuary habitats grant safari benefits. Examples:
- Zebra habitat → movement upgrade
- Elephant habitat → special obstacle interaction
- Giraffe habitat → reveals hidden discoveries

**Rule:** at most **one** simple bond in the first version, and only if playtesting shows it helps. Passive bonuses must never replace skill.
Status: 🟡 idea, not confirmed.
