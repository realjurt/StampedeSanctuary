# Game Vision

Back to [[00 - Project Dashboard]]

## One sentence
A colorful, voxel-inspired Roblox game where players ride wild animals through safari environments, leap between animals, discover new species, and bring them home to build their own animal sanctuary.

## Two experiences that reinforce each other
- **Action (Safari):** riding, jumping, dodging, switching animals, discovering species. Must be fun with no rewards attached.
- **Progression (Sanctuary):** collecting, habitats, coins, upgrades, unlocks. Provides ownership and long-term goals.

## Design principles (confirmed)
1. Understandable within seconds.
2. First gameplay interaction happens fast.
3. Riding is responsive and satisfying.
4. Switching animals is exciting.
5. Animals play meaningfully differently.
6. Clear short- and long-term goals.
7. Rewarding progression without heavy grinding.
8. Consistent, memorable visuals.
9. Works on desktop and mobile.
10. Multiplayer-ready without complicating the prototype.

## Originality rule
Inspired by Rodeo Stampede's action + collection combo. **Never** copy its maps, models, branding, UI, music, or code. Learn principles; build our own identity.

## Game feel
Cheerful · playful · responsive · colorful · rewarding · lighthearted · easy to understand · replayable. Safari = energetic. Sanctuary = calm.
